## English

Short description:

```text
Adds an MP3 button under YouTube videos. One click sends the video to the free Tunegrab app for Windows.
```

Description:

```text
Tunegrab puts an MP3 button right next to Like and Share on YouTube.
Click it and the free Tunegrab app for Windows saves the audio as an MP3 with title, artist and cover art.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Install the free Tunegrab app for Windows: https://github.com/BukazLucky/tunegrab
2. Open any YouTube video and click MP3.
3. The first time, your browser asks whether to open Tunegrab. Tick "Always allow".

ALSO WORKS
• Shorts and playlists (a floating MP3 button in the corner)
• Right-click any YouTube link → "Download MP3 with Tunegrab"
• The Tunegrab icon in the toolbar
• 11 languages

PRIVACY
The extension collects no data. It only passes the address of the page you clicked to the Tunegrab app on your
computer. No servers, no analytics, no accounts. Open source: https://github.com/BukazLucky/tunegrab

IMPORTANT
The extension doesn't download anything by itself; it needs the Tunegrab app for Windows.
Only download content you have the right to download. Not affiliated with YouTube or Google.
```
